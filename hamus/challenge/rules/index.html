Hamus community challenges · sponsored by Wild Beles LLC · last updated July 9, 2026

NO PURCHASE NECESSARY. Entering is completely free — a free Hamus account is all you need. A purchase does not improve your chances of winning. Void where prohibited by law.

1 · Sponsor

Challenges are sponsored and administered by Wild Beles LLC ("Hamus"), Dallas–Fort Worth, Texas. Contact: through the Hamus app (Settings → Help) or the contact options at wildbeles.com/hamus.

2 · Eligibility

  • Open to Hamus account holders who are 18 years or older.
  • Anyone eligible may enter and win the challenge title, wherever they live.
  • Physical prizes are handed off in person in the Dallas–Fort Worth, Texas area. If the winner is outside the DFW area, they receive the winner title and a feature on Hamus and @hamusapp instead of the physical prize. No cash substitution; prizes are not transferable.
  • Wild Beles LLC personnel and challenge judges (where applicable) are not eligible for prizes.

3 · How to enter

  • Open the challenge in the Hamus app and post your entry photo before the deadline shown in the app (the in-app countdown is authoritative; times are US Central Time).
  • One entry per person. Entries must be your own work/photo and follow the Hamus Terms of Service — no religious or political content, nothing offensive, nothing you don't have the right to post.

4 · Winner selection & notification

  • The entry with the most community votes in the Hamus app when the challenge closes wins. Ties are broken in favor of the earlier entry.
  • Votes from fake, duplicate, or automated accounts don't count. Hamus may disqualify entries or votes it reasonably believes are fraudulent, and may remove entries that violate the Terms.
  • The winner is announced in the app and notified via their Hamus account. If a winner doesn't respond within 7 days, the prize may go to the runner-up.

5 · Prize

The prize for each challenge is described on the challenge page, with an approximate retail value under $100 unless stated otherwise. Prize details, including any geographic handoff restriction, are stated on the challenge page before entry.
