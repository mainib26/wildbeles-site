Delete Your Account

Last updated: June 26, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Hamus account (Hamus is operated by Wild Beles LLC) and what happens to your data when you do. You can delete your account directly in the app, or request deletion by email.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Hamus app and sign in.
  2. Go to the You (profile) tab.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

If you can't access the app

Email hamusapp@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my account.” We may ask you to verify ownership of the account before we process the request.

What gets deleted

When you delete your account, it is deactivated immediately and your profile and listings are removed from public view. Within a 90-day recovery window (so an accidental deletion can be undone), the following personal data is permanently deleted or de-identified:

  • Your profile (name, email, phone, avatar, bio, language and location preferences)
  • Your listings and item details, and photos you uploaded
  • Your saved items and preferences
  • Your messages to other users
  • Your account login credentials

What may be kept

  • Legally required records: limited information we must retain to resolve disputes, prevent fraud and abuse, or comply with law — kept in minimal or de-identified form, as described in our Privacy Policy.
  • Public or re-shared content: content you made public (for example, reviews or published Hamus Learn lessons) and content other users re-shared may persist after your account is deleted.

Questions

For anything about account deletion or your data, contact us at hamusapp@gmail.com. For full details on how we handle your information, see our Privacy Policy.
