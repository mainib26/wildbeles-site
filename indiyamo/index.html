Meet Indy

Your personal travel co-pilot

Indy isn't a generic chatbot. She reads your actual trip — the dates, the party size, the open votes, the tasks nobody's touched yet — and tells you exactly what needs to happen next.

Knows your trip cold

Indy reads your itinerary, budget, open votes, and overdue tasks before every response. No context-setting required.

Takes action, not just advice

Ask Indy to add a stop, create a group vote, or set a task reminder — she does it, with one confirmation tap.

Proactive, not reactive

Indy surfaces what matters before you ask — a stale vote, a passport expiry, a budget that's crept past mid-range.

Indy
