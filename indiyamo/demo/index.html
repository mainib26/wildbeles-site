The 2-minute walkthrough — solo travelers, families, and groups going from vague plans to a real trip with Indy, the proactive AI copilot.

The problem

Planning a trip sounds fun. Actually getting one over the line isn't.

Trips fall apart in group chats, saved links, half-made decisions, missing details, and "we'll figure it out later." One person usually ends up doing the work, but consumer travel tools still behave like search engines, not copilots.

What Indiyamo does

Indiyamo doesn't just answer travel questions. It helps finish the trip.

Instead of dumping links, notes, and decisions across five apps, travelers get one place to track readiness, surface what's missing, and let Indy guide the next step.

01

Tracks the messy middle

Dates, people, budget, transport, lodging, documents, activities, and loose ends live together instead of getting scattered across tabs, notes, and text threads.

02

Guides the next step

Indy helps the planner see what's missing, asks the next right question, and moves the trip forward step by step so planning feels guided instead of fragmented.

03

Knows trip readiness

Computer helped build Indy around the readiness model: read the current trip state, identify open risks, and turn vague planning chaos into concrete action.

Why this matters

Most travel tools help you search. Very few help you coordinate, decide, and follow through.

That gap gets worse for families and groups, where one missed detail can stall the whole trip. Indiyamo is built for that messy middle between "we should go somewhere" and "we're ready."
